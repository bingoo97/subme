# Panel access tiers — local module

Local implementation only. Automation defaults to OFF; no hosting deployment was performed. Settings → Admin Mode → Panel payment contains independent automation and receipt-email switches. With automation OFF, the existing external renewal URL and manual paid-through date remain available. Receipt emails default ON but no payment/email processing runs while automation is OFF.

## Pricing and usage

Default monthly prices in the panel's selected currency are 29 / 59 / 99. Tier 1 includes up to 50 paid subscriptions, Tier 2 up to 250, Tier 3 above 250. Each paid subscription counts separately, including multiple orders belonging to the same customer. Prices and the first two limits are configurable. Tier 3 has no upper limit. Currency changes require saving tier prices again; existing invoice currency/rate snapshots do not change.

A durable paid-through ledger is separate from the order status. It captures initial paid orders, linked approved bank/crypto payments, paid renewals pending activation and applied renewals. It retains the greatest paid-through date and has no cascading foreign key. Changing status, clearing payment flags, shortening dates, reversing acceptance, or deleting an order cannot erase an already recorded entitlement. Entries stop counting at their natural paid-through date. Product credits and unpaid subscriptions do not count. Existing history is backfilled when the module is installed and before pricing; order update/delete/reversal/renewal paths capture evidence around changes.

The ±10% threshold zones (45–55 and 225–275 with defaults) allow only one month. Otherwise choose one or three months. More than 30 days remaining blocks a new invoice. A unique open-slot constraint and transactional config-row lock allow one outstanding payment across administrators, tabs and concurrent requests.

This is application-level protection against manipulating the order workflow, not DRM against a server/database owner. An owner who can edit Admin Mode prices, disable automation, change the licence date manually, alter SQL or replace code remains trusted.

## Crypto payments

Configured receivers only: BTC mainnet, SOL mainnet, DOGE mainnet and USDT Ethereum ERC-20. Wallet syntax/checksums are validated where applicable. Each installation must use its own receiving addresses, exclusively for panel access. Customer-wallet overlap is excluded and configuring those panel receivers as customer wallets is rejected. Dedicated per-installation addresses are essential for identifying partial payments; an arbitrary transfer to a shared global merchant wallet cannot safely identify its tenant.

Rates use the existing shared crypto cache. A new invoice requires a fresh rate (maximum 30 minutes old), locks price/currency/receiver/network/rate and includes a tiny identifying amount in the displayed crypto total. QR is generated locally by vendored qrcode-generator (MIT), contains the address only and does not transmit payment data to a QR service. The UI shows the exact copyable amount, address, network, locked rate and payment deadline. Copy buttons turn green with a check and “Copied” for five seconds, also highlighting the copied value. Unchanged polling does not clear this feedback. A waiting spinner indicates pending verification; manual payment checks scroll to the status notice, while background polling never moves the view. The QR is about 206 px on large desktop screens, 174 px on smaller desktop/tablet screens and up to 246 px on phones, shrinking to fit narrow displays. An odd last coin card spans both columns.

An unpaid invoice can be cancelled with “Back - change payment method”; after its send deadline the message screen offers “Cancel payment and go back”. All configured coins stay visible. Choosing a coin after conscious cancellation always creates a fresh invoice, including the same coin: new identifier, current usable rate, distinct identifying amount, selected period and a new 30-minute deadline. Cancelled invoices retain their original finality monitoring. Exact unique quoted amounts cannot be claimed by another invoice; non-exact transfers across overlapping monitored quotes require review. On-time observations are not reassigned to a fresh invoice. Confirmed amounts and observed transfers prevent cancellation. Late receipts on cancelled invoices are retained for review, not silently credited to a new invoice. Known unresolved late receipts suppress another payment screen. All scanners share the same installation-wide lease.

At 00:00 the modal removes the QR, copyable payment details and spinner, shows a separate receipt-monitoring message, and keeps automatic status checks. With no receipt evidence, cancel-and-return is available as soon as the send deadline ends. Finality expiry without evidence returns to method/period selection; an existing observation remains reviewable and blocks duplicate payment. Desktop recipient addresses use 12–13px; action buttons use a consistent 10px gap and gray outlined return control.

Initial payment window: 30 minutes. A red MM:SS countdown sits beside the deadline, uses the server clock reference and ticks locally without HTTP/SQL traffic. It stops at 00:00; pending network confirmations remain under the existing bounded finality checks. Hidden/closed modals pause the clock and recalculate from the absolute deadline when shown again. Network confirmations are checked for up to 48 hours. A confirmed underpayment below 90% is recorded and shows the remainder to the full invoice amount; its top-up window extends once to 48 hours from invoice creation, with another 24 hours for finality. No new invoice is offered meanwhile. At 90–100% of the requested crypto total, grant the proportional number of whole days, rounded down. At or above 100%, grant the full quoted period, never more. Closed paid invoices are not credited again by later transfers. Transfers are deduplicated independently by network/hash and customer-deposit claims are excluded.

Full period length is the sum of the lengths of the selected base calendar month(s): January 31 days, February 28/29, etc. Access starts after the current inclusive paid-through date, or today when expired. Example: 99% of a 30-day period grants 29 days; 90% of a 31-day period grants 27 days. Dates are advanced in calendar days in the runtime timezone, including DST transitions. Invoice duration is fixed at creation; settlement adds the awarded days to the then-current base date.

Only verified incoming transfers qualify for access. Transfers timestamped in the original payment window can confirm later within the finality window. The existing bounded scanner also retains a unique first-seen observation for incoming transactions visible before the deadline. If such a transaction is mined after the deadline, its original observation preserves eligibility after full network verification. Observations never credit money/days, cannot be reassigned to another invoice and are inserted only once; no extra external API calls are added. A verified late transfer with no on-time evidence is retained for owner review and never silently discarded or automatically credited. The 00:00 notice switches immediately in the browser to confirmation-waiting guidance, removes the instruction to send funds and keeps normal polling active. Existing verifiers require BTC 2 confirmations, DOGE 6, SOL finalized successful native transfers, USDT successful Ethereum receipts with 12 confirmations and the allowlisted token contract/destination. No fuzzy fit score, wallet balance or earlier transaction is used for automatic credit. Multiple confirmed top-ups are summed atomically. Growth into a higher tier, a newly restricted three-month period, or changed licence eligibility puts a received payment into review instead of granting underpriced access. Expired partial payments also remain in review. Admin Mode shows the last ten payments and a review-close action that adds no days, preserving the received transfers for manual reconciliation.

The paid-through update, transfer claims, awarded days and queued email share one transaction. Duplicate processing cannot extend twice or send duplicate receipts. Notification is addressed to the active account with login `admin`, using its current email, and includes requested/received amounts, network, transaction, awarded/full days and new date. Partial-payment notices include the outstanding crypto amount. SMTP remains in the existing email worker.

The existing maintenance switch is preserved after renewal: the administrator can reopen the customer site when ready; an unrelated manual maintenance setting is never silently cleared.

## Load and tests

Ordinary page loads do not fetch market prices or scan blockchain history for this module. Opening the payment modal synchronizes usage at most once per minute. Creation recalculates it transactionally. Scans share a database lease, at most one invoice per 60 seconds per installation, an 8-request / 12-second transport budget, and existing host caches/backoff. An existing invoice gets its first automatic status check two seconds after opening; new invoices and normal checks use a 60-second interval. Hidden tabs, offline tabs and closed modals send no polling traffic. Temporary fetch/provider failures retry automatically with exponential backoff from one to five minutes; session/CSRF/permission errors stop polling. Success, expiry and review stop polling as appropriate. The manual “Refresh status” button is optional. The existing maintenance worker independently checks invoices even when the modal/browser is closed. A fresh shared scan lease short-circuits before opening a transaction, avoiding duplicate row locks and network requests across worker and browser tabs. OFF performs no blockchain scanning.

Regression suite: `dashboard-panel/tests/panel_tiers_regression.php`, guarded to disposable `_test_` databases and a separate crypto cache. Covers thresholds, dates/leap years, monotonic financial history, deleted/status-toggled orders, renewals, price/receiver validation, concurrent invoice creation, permissions/CSRF/method, strict finality/time/direction validation, underpayments/top-ups, duplicate settlement, proportional days, tier growth and email deduplication/toggle. No real transfers or external inboxes are used. The real email worker is exercised against an isolated loopback SMTP server, including delivery, receipt contents, deduplication and retry after connection failure. Existing subscription, order-extension, notification and full-page render suites are also run.

QR library: https://github.com/kazuhikoarase/qrcode-generator — source bundled in `public_html/assets/vendor/qrcode-generator/`, with its MIT licence. No new package runtime or hosted checkout service is required.
